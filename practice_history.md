## [2026-11-19 03:32:09] Opinion (Agree / Disagree) — Introduction (Initial Draft)

**Topic:** Some people think that spending money on space exploration is a waste of financial resources that should be used to solve problems on Earth. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

**Word Count:** 44 words

### Your Draft
```text
Some people believe that money spent on space exploration should instead be used to solve problems on Earth. I partly agree with this view because issues such as poverty and healthcare need urgent attention. However, space exploration can also bring important benefits to humanity.
```

### Gemini Review & Changes
Estimated Band: 6.5

Main Issues:
- Task Response: "Partly agree" splits the thesis into two opposing directions rather than choosing a clear stance with two supporting ideas.
- Coherence and Cohesion: The second previewed idea ("important benefits") is too vague and lacks a specific anchor for the body paragraphs.
- Grammar/Style: A Band 7.5+ intro should preview two distinct, concrete reasons rather than a vague contrast.

Suggested Corrections:
1. "Although pressing terrestrial issues like poverty require immediate financial investment, I completely disagree that funding space exploration is a waste of resources because it drives technological innovation and secures our long-term survival." — (clear stance with two distinct ideas)
2. "While some argue that public funds should strictly target problems on Earth, I disagree with this perspective, as space research yields critical medical advancements and creates vital economic growth." — (stronger vocabulary and precise previewing)
